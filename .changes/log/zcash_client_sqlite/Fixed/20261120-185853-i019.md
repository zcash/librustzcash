The wallet now records the sending account for the outputs of a transaction
that it stored before learning that one of its accounts funded it.
Previously `v_tx_outputs.from_account_uuid` and
`v_received_outputs.sent_note_id` stayed `NULL` for such outputs, so a
transfer between two accounts of one wallet appeared as a receipt from an
unknown sender. A migration corrects the transactions that an existing
wallet already stores.

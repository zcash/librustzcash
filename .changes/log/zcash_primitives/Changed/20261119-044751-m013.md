`zcash_primitives` makes Sapling and Orchard support optional through the
`sapling` and `orchard` features. With default features disabled, enable the
features for the typed protocols you use; byte parsing requires neither.
The `circuits` feature enables both.

Downstream implementations of `transaction::{Authorization, TransactionDigest}`
and callers of feature-dependent positional APIs, including
`TransactionData::from_parts*`, must enable both `sapling` and `orchard`
to remain compatible with Cargo feature unification.

`zcash_primitives::transaction::TransactionData::digest`, `zcash_primitives::transaction::Transaction::auth_commitment`, and `zcash_primitives::transaction::sighash::PrecomputedSighashData::{new, new_zip244}` now take a `BundleDigestProvider` and return a `Result`. Pass `&()` when no external bundle digests are available. Under NU8, `TransactionDigest` implementations must handle retained effecting and authorizing bundles.

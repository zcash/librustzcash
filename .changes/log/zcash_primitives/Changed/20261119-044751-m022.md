`zcash_primitives::transaction::TransactionDigest::digest_ironwood` takes a
leading `TxVersion` argument.

With `zcash_unstable="nu8"`, implementations must also provide the
`CoinbaseDigest` and `ValuePoolDeltasDigest` associated types and
`digest_coinbase` and `digest_value_pool_deltas` methods. `combine` takes
the two additional digest arguments.

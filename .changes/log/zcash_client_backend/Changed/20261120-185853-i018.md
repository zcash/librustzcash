Without the `transparent-inputs` feature,
`zcash_client_backend::data_api::ll::wallet::store_decrypted_tx` now resolves
the recipient of a sent transparent output with
`LowLevelWalletRead::select_receiving_address`, as it does with the feature
enabled. `LowLevelWalletWrite::put_sent_output` may therefore receive a
unified address where it previously received a transparent address.

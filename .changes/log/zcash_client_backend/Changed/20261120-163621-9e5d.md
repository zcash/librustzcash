`zcash_client_backend::fees::zip317::MultiOutputChangeStrategy`, when its `SplitPolicy`
sets no minimum split output value, counts notes worth more than 100 times the ZIP 317
marginal fee in effect at the target height as usable when deciding how to split change
(100000 zatoshis once the ZIP 2009 fee reduction is in effect). Previously the threshold
was always 500000 zatoshis.
